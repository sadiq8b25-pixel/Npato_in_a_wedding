📖 The rules

Objective

Each round a random letter drops. Be fastest to fill all four blanks — Name, Place, Animal, Thing — with words starting with that letter, then slam STOP!. Everyone's pens drop instantly.

Scoring

Unique word · 10Someone wrote the same · 5Blank / wrong letter / voided · 0

The buzzer

STOP only arms when all four of your words are filled and start with the round letter. If the clock hits zero first, whatever you've written is submitted automatically.

Challenges 🚩

At the reveal, flag any opponent's word you think is fake. When a majority of the room flags it, it's stamped VOID and scores 0. Un-flag to change your vote — scores update live.

Fair play locks 🛡️

  • Answers are hidden until pens drop — live progress shows counts only.
  • The host's clock is authoritative; late submissions are rejected.
  • Letters are random and never repeat within a match.
  • Paste & drop are blocked. One submission per round, then it's locked.
  • Leaving the tab mid-round earns a 👀 badge at the reveal.

The host

The host's device is the referee — it stores the room, runs the clock and scores the words. Pick someone you trust 🤝. When the host closes the room, all storage is erased.
